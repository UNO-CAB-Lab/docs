# Sharing Your Survey

#### Creating a Qualtrics Account

If you don't have an account already, you can create one for free by visiting [<mark style="color:$primary;">https://www.qualtrics.com/free-account/</mark>](https://www.qualtrics.com/free-account/).

If you're currently affiliated with the University of Nebraska System, you can request your Qualtrics account [<mark style="color:$primary;">here</mark>](https://nusupport.nebraska.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/Requests/Service/69/Qualtrics-Survey-Tool).

#### Adding a Collaborator

Before we can run your study, you'll need to add the CAB Lab as a collaborator in Qualtrics.

1. Email the CAB Lab at <mark style="color:$primary;">unocablab@unomaha.edu</mark> indicating that you're ready to share your Qualtrics project. The Lab staff will give you a username to enter in Step 5.
2. From the Qualtrics landing page, navigate to 'Projects.'
3. Locate the survey you'd like to share with the Lab.
4. Click the three dots on the far right, then choose 'Collaborate.'
5. Type the username given to you in the box and select the user.
6. Make sure all the boxes are checked before clicking 'Save.'

This is **not** to give us credit for your work— it's simply the only way we can pair our sensor data with your Qualtrics survey data.

Researchers have the final say in how their Qualtrics surveys are designed. The CAB Lab will never delete or modify question content without researcher consent. If you have questions about the steps we take to pair our data with Qualtrics, please feel free to reach out to us.&#x20;
